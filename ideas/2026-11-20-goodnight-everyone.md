# Goodnight, everyone

An original sleepy night meadow with six big creatures in their beds. The child taps one and it yawns, the voice says "goodnight, cow", and then "bonne nuit, la vache" when French is on. The creature pulls up its blanket and its window goes dark. There is nothing to get wrong: a tap on a sleeping creature only makes a soft snore. When all six are asleep, the moon comes up, the meadow goes quiet and the end screen shows.
