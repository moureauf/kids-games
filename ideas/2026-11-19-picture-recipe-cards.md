# Picture recipe cards

An original bear bakes with the child from picture cards: "2 scoops of flour, 3 berries". The child taps the cards in order and counts each item into the bowl, which says each number. A wrong count just lets the bear wait and point at the card. After one cake the bear shares it with the toys and the kitchen light goes off.
