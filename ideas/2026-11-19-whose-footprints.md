# Whose footprints?

A trail of big footprints (hooves, webbed feet, tyre tracks, dinosaur toes) crosses the meadow. The child taps a print and it glows; the trail leads to one of three creatures peeking out from behind bushes, and tapping the one that made it brings it out with its name said aloud (any tap on another creature just gets a friendly wave and its name too, so the toddler cannot fail and the older child learns to match the shape). The round is six trails, then the creatures all walk home along their own tracks and the meadow goes quiet.
