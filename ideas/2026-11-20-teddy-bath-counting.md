# Bubble bath for the toys

An original teddy, duck and robot sit in a big bath. The child taps the bubble jar and one big bubble floats onto a toy; the voice counts each bubble aloud ("one, two, three"). Each toy wants a number of bubbles shown as dots on a little card, and when it has enough it giggles and waves. Extra bubbles just pop softly, so nothing goes wrong. After five toys the plug comes out, the water swirls away, and the toys wrap up in towels to sleep.
