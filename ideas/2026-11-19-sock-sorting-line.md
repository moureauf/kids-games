# Sock sorting line

A washing line with original odd socks (stripes, spots, stars). Drag each sock to its matching pair; a matched pair says its pattern and colour ("two spotty blue socks"). A wrong sock gently swings back. After 8 pairs the basket is empty and the line goes still.
