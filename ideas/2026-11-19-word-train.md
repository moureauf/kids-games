# Word train

A little train pulls three carriages, and each carriage carries one Set 1 letter (s, a, t). The child taps the carriages from left to right: each one says its pure sound, then the train toots and says the whole word while a picture of it rides on the last carriage. Taps out of order just wobble the carriage and point to the first one. After five words the train reaches its station, the driver waves, and the lights go down.
