# Train carriage numbers

An original toy train waits at a station with its carriages out of order, each with a big number painted on its side. The child drags the carriages onto the track so the numbers go 1, 2, 3 up to 10 (later to 20), and the voice says each number as it clicks into place. A carriage in the wrong place rolls gently back with a hint ("What comes after 4?"). When all ten carriages are joined, the train toots once and slowly puffs away to the engine shed for the night.
