# Peekaboo barn doors

A big original barn has three huge doors. Tap a door and it swings open slowly: an animal or a vehicle peeps out, makes its sound and the voice says its name ("hen", then "la poule" when French is on). Tap again and the door closes with a soft "peekaboo". There is nothing to get wrong. After eight doors the farmer's lamp goes out, the animals curl up in the straw, and the barn goes to sleep.
