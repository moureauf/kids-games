# French picnic basket

An original picnic blanket with a basket in the middle. A friendly hedgehog asks for one food at a time, first in English and then in French with the article ("an apple ... une pomme"), and the child taps the matching picture to drop it into the basket; a wrong picture just wobbles and the hedgehog says the word again. It only plays when the French words setting is on. After eight foods the basket is full, the hedgehog shares the picnic with a friend, and they lie down to watch the clouds.
