# Fairy tea party

Two or three fairies sit round a toadstool table and a plate of cakes arrives. The child drags one cake at a time onto each fairy's plate; when the plates are even, the fairies clap and count their cakes together out loud ("one, two, three each!"). If one fairy has more, she just looks at her friend's empty plate and waits. Six plates of cakes (up to 12 cakes), then the fairies yawn, stack the cups and fly home to bed.
