# Rainy window tracing

Raindrops run down an original steamy window, and the child traces the pre-writing patterns they leave: lines down, zig-zags, waves and loops, from the start dot. Each finished pattern turns into a little rainbow on the glass. After six patterns the rain stops and the sun dries the window.
