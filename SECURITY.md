# Security and privacy

## What the site does with data

- The site has no server, no accounts and no tracking.
- After it loads, it does not send data over the network.
- Settings and recordings stay in the browser storage on the device.

## Report a problem

If you find a security or privacy problem, do not open a public issue.

1. Go to the **Security** tab of the repository.
2. Select **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Write what you found and how to see it.

The owner replies as soon as possible.

## Privacy rules for contributors

- Do not put the names, photos or voices of the children in the repo,
  issues, pull requests, commit messages or screenshots.
- Do not add analytics, trackers or calls to other servers.
- Secret scanning and push protection are on for this repository.
